\textbf{Source and statement.}
Conjecture 3 of Huynh-Vu, Zaw and Scarani~[1] asserts that, for every spin
ensemble and every odd $K\geq3$, a score above $T_K$ certifies genuine
multipartite entanglement (GME). In units $\hbar=1$, for a finite ensemble
of positive half-integer spins $j_1,\ldots,j_N$, write
\[
J_\alpha=\sum_{n=1}^{N}J_\alpha^{(n)},\quad
J_k=\cos\theta_k J_x+\sin\theta_k J_y,\quad
\theta_k=\frac{2\pi k}{K},\quad
Q_K=\frac1K\sum_{k=0}^{K-1}\pos(J_k).
\]
For a Hermitian matrix $A$, $\pos(A)$ has spectral weights $1,1/2,0$
on positive, zero, and negative eigenvalues, respectively. A density matrix
$\rho\succeq0$, $\tr\rho=1$, has score $P_K(\rho)=\tr(\rho Q_K)$.
A state separable across a nonempty proper subset $S$ is a convex mixture
of $\rho_S\otimes\rho_{S^c}$. GME excludes convex mixtures of such states,
allowing different cuts in different terms. With
$c_K=2^{-(K-1)}\binom{K-1}{(K-1)/2}$, the proposed threshold is
\[
T_K=\begin{cases}
23/32,&K=3,\\
(69+\sqrt{181})/128,&K=5,\\
\frac12\left(1+c_K\frac{K-1}{K+1}\right),&K\geq7\text{ odd}.
\end{cases}
\]

\textbf{Score-preserving padding.}
Let $s=(\ket{\uparrow\downarrow}-\ket{\downarrow\uparrow})/\sqrt2$ be
the normalized two-spin-$1/2$ singlet. The pair operators
$L_\alpha=J_\alpha^{(1)}+J_\alpha^{(2)}$ satisfy $L_xs=L_ys=0$:
applying either spin-$1/2$ matrix to the two terms gives cancelling coefficients.
For any remaining finite ensemble, let $B_k$ be its precession observable
and $A_k=L_k\otimes\id+\id\otimes B_k$ the padded observable.
The isometry $V\varphi=s\otimes\varphi$ satisfies $A_kV=VB_k$.
If $B_k u=\lambda u$, then $A_k Vu=\lambda Vu$, so
$\pos(A_k)Vu=V\pos(B_k)u$. An eigenbasis of $B_k$ therefore gives
$\pos(A_k)V=V\pos(B_k)$, including the half weight at zero.
For every remaining density matrix $\sigma$, cyclicity of the finite trace gives
\[
\tr\!\left[(\ket{s}\bra{s}\otimes\sigma)\pos(A_k)\right]
=\tr\!\left[\sigma V^*\pos(A_k)V\right]
=\tr[\sigma\pos(B_k)].
\]
Averaging over $k$ proves $P_K(\ket{s}\bra{s}\otimes\sigma)=P_K(\sigma)$.
The padded state is a product across the pair--rest cut and hence is not GME.

\textbf{Consequence for unrestricted ensembles.}
For fixed $K$, let $\mathcal S_K^{\rm q}$ be all scores from all nonempty
finite positive-spin ensembles, and let $\mathcal S_K^{\rm bisep}$ restrict
to biseparable states on ensembles with at least two particles. Padding
realizes every member of $\mathcal S_K^{\rm q}$ in
$\mathcal S_K^{\rm bisep}$; the reverse inclusion is immediate. Thus
$\mathcal S_K^{\rm q}=\mathcal S_K^{\rm bisep}$ and their suprema agree.
Any composition-independent threshold below this supremum admits a
biseparable violation. This argument permits changing the ensemble;
it does not identify the two optima for a fixed ensemble.

\textbf{An exact three-particle counterexample at $K=3$.}
Take physical spins $(1/2,1/2,3/2)$ and the pure state
\[
\psi=s\otimes g,\qquad
g=\frac{\ket{3/2,3/2}-\ket{3/2,-3/2}}{\sqrt2},\qquad
\rho=\ket{\psi}\bra{\psi}.
\]
Both factors are unit vectors, so $\rho$ is positive semidefinite with
trace one. Its displayed factorization proves separability across
$\{1,2\}\mid\{3\}$. All three constituent spins are strictly positive.

Here is a full finite calculation of the remaining spin's score. In the
ordered $J_z$ basis with $m=3/2,1/2,-1/2,-3/2$, put $a=\sqrt3$ and
\[
X=J_x^{(3/2)}=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&a/2&0&0\\ a/2&0&1&0\\0&1&0&a/2\\0&0&a/2&0
\end{pmatrix},\qquad
W=\begin{pmatrix}
1&a&a&1\\a&1&-1&-a\\a&-1&-1&a\\1&-a&a&-1
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Direct multiplication gives $W^*W=8\id$ and
\[
XW=W\operatorname{diag}(3/2,1/2,-1/2,-3/2).
\]
Consequently
\[
\pos(X)=\frac18W\operatorname{diag}(1,1,0,0)W^*
=\begin{pmatrix}
1/2&a/4&0&-1/4\\a/4&1/2&1/4&0\\
0&1/4&1/2&a/4\\-1/4&0&a/4&1/2
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
For $X_\theta=\cos\theta J_x^{(3/2)}+\sin\theta J_y^{(3/2)}$, let
$R_\theta=\operatorname{diag}(1,e^{i\theta},e^{2i\theta},e^{3i\theta})$.
The standard spin matrices give
$X_\theta=R_\theta X R_\theta^*$ and therefore
$\pos(X_\theta)=R_\theta\pos(X)R_\theta^*$.
The endpoint diagonal entries are $1/2$ and the endpoint off-diagonal
entry is $-e^{-3i\theta}/4$. Since $g=(1,0,0,-1)^T/\sqrt2$,
\[
\bra{g}\pos(X_\theta)\ket{g}
=\frac12+\frac14\cos(3\theta).
\]
For each $\theta_k=2\pi k/3$, this equals $3/4$.
Padding therefore gives
\[
\boxed{P_3(\rho)=\frac34=\frac{24}{32}>\frac{23}{32}=T_3,
\qquad \rho\text{ is biseparable}.}
\]
This refutes Conjecture 3 with exact gap $1/32$. It also violates the
implication in Result 4: $K=3$ lies in its range, the constituent-spin sum
is $5/2\leq15$, and $1/32>10^{-11}$.

\textbf{What survives.}
The witness lies outside Result 1's hypothesis $\sum_n j_n=K/2$;
that source result is not contradicted. Nor does it contradict a bound
whose hypotheses require positive effective spins on both sides of a cut.
The essential distinction is that positive \emph{constituent} spins
can have an effective spin-zero sector. Omitting that sector from a
maximization over cuts omits physically admissible biseparable states.

\textbf{A uniform family for every odd $K\geq3$.}
Let $L=(K-1)/2$ and take $K$ remaining spin-$1/2$ particles in
\[
g_K=\frac{\ket{\uparrow}^{\otimes K}+(-1)^L
\ket{\downarrow}^{\otimes K}}{\sqrt2}.
\]
For $B_0=\frac12\sum_{n=1}^{K}\sigma_x^{(n)}$, its tensor-product
$x$ eigenbasis is indexed by the number $r$ of negative local eigenvalues.
The eigenvalue is $K/2-r$, which is positive exactly when $0\leq r\leq L$.
Each eigenvector contributes $2^{-K}$ to the endpoint diagonal entries
and $(-1)^r2^{-K}$ to their off-diagonal entry. Hence those diagonal
entries are $1/2$, and
\[
\bra{\uparrow^K}\pos(B_0)\ket{\downarrow^K}
=2^{-K}\sum_{r=0}^{L}(-1)^r\binom Kr
=(-1)^L2^{-K}\binom{K-1}{L}
=\frac{(-1)^L c_K}{2}.
\]
The middle identity follows from Pascal's rule: the two alternating
sums telescope, leaving $(-1)^L\binom{K-1}{L}$.
Rotation about $z$ multiplies the endpoint off-diagonal entry by
$e^{-iK\theta}$; for $\theta_k=2\pi k/K$ this is one.
Thus every sampled direction has the same expectation and
\[
P_K(g_K)=\frac{1+c_K}{2},\qquad
P_K(s\otimes g_K)-T_K=
\begin{cases}
1/32,&K=3,\\
(19-\sqrt{181})/128,&K=5,\\
c_K/(K+1),&K\geq7\text{ odd}.
\end{cases}
\]
All gaps are strictly positive: $181<19^2$ and $c_K>0$.
The padded state has $K+2$ positive-spin constituents and is biseparable
across the singlet--rest cut. This is an exact written proof of the family,
not an inference from finitely many numerical samples.

\textbf{What the Lean development checks.}
The linked module~[2] defines the literal tensor-product spin observables,
the spectral convention at zero, normalized density matrices,
cut separability, GME, and the universal implication. Its public theorem
\texttt{result : $\neg$ claim} proves the three-particle $K=3$ refutation above.
Its private helper \texttt{singlet\_padding\_ensemble\_score}, used in that
proof, checks the score identity for an arbitrary remaining spin list and
matrix, with the literal ensemble observables. The spin parameters in
Lean are doubled physical spins: \texttt{![1,1,3]} denotes
$(1/2,1/2,3/2)$.

The all-odd GHZ formula and the score-set/supremum consequence proved
here are not separate Lean theorems in this module. The merged delivery
records a successful Lean build and standard axiom closure
(\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, \texttt{Quot.sound}) at the
revision linked below; this note reports that validation, not a fresh run.
Whether an appropriate exclusion of zero-spin cut sectors restores the
printed threshold remains an open corrected question.
